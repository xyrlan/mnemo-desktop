Nexus v2, wave 1: four pieces. The Nexus becomes a full-window **ship laid out by attention**:
- a bridge of exceptions, where you are;
- one deck per repo, with rooms for in flight, hangar and stranded;
- captains at consoles for parents, robots for children;
- a ship's log;
- what you do from it: answer, merge, land, stop, message, dispatch.

**Read `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-nexus-v2-design.md` first.** It holds every decision, the
numbers behind them and what each figure is computed from. **Run the prototype**
(`docs/superpowers/prototypes/nexus-v2/index.html`, with `capture.py` writing its `fleet.js` from
this machine) and look at `attention-*.png` beside it. The prototype is the reference for behaviour,
wording and numbers; build it the app's way. Where this contract and the spec disagree, ask the
parent session.

**Every seam is already on `main`**, so each piece compiles and tests alone, and the four merge in
any order. Nobody writes a stand-in for another piece's signature.
- `src/nexus/ship-types.ts` holds every shared type: the feed's, the ship's, the log's, the actions'
  and every component's props. **No piece edits it.**
- `src/nexus/NexusView.tsx` is the full-window view and its wiring:
  - reads: the snapshot, the fleet's fused agent states, the pulse, the feed polled every 3 s while
    shown;
  - builds the ship and appends the log;
  - keys: Tab triage, ⌥1–9, `/` focusing `[data-fleet-prompt] input`, Esc clearing the selection;
  - opens a figure the way v1 did, until the drawer lands.

  It is joined by `view.tsx` (the app opens on it; `nexus.toggle` is ⌘0), `store.ts`,
  `ship-client.ts` (`asksFrom`, `tauriShip`) and `env.ts`. No piece edits them.
- `src-tauri/src/nexus_feed.rs`: the feed's serde types and `nexus_feed` with a stub body,
  **registered in `lib.rs`**. Its JSON matches `ship-types.ts` (tested).

## feed

- **files:** src-tauri/src/nexus_feed.rs, src-tauri/src/nexus_feed/
- **exposes:** `nexus_feed(asks: Vec<FeedAsk>) -> Feed`
- **consumes:** nothing
- **model:** opus
- **effort:** high

What the snapshot does not carry (spec, "What each figure is computed from"), for every `FeedAsk`
the view sends every 3 s.

- **The transcript:** `crate::mission::transcript_path(cwd, session_id)` finds it on any account.
  - Read only what was appended since the last call. A poll over 15 sessions must not re-read whole
    transcripts.
  - From it come:
    - `activity`: the last tool call. `running` while its `tool_result` has not arrived.
    - `said`: the last assistant text.
    - `context`: the last API call's `input + cache_read + cache_creation`, with a message's usage
      counted once per message id.
    - `model`: the last assistant message's.
    - `ask`: an `AskUserQuestion` still unanswered.
    - `topic`: for a parent, its latest prompt typed by a person. Not a tool result, a command
      wrapper or a system reminder.
- **The job:** the snapshot's `intent` is only the first line. The full one, in
  `<the account's config dir>/jobs/<jobId>/state.json`, gives `contract`: the feature, the piece,
  and every piece it writes against (the intent's wording is quoted in the spec).
- **PR fates:**
  - Every `prUrl` gives state, `mergedAt`, `closedAt` and each check of the last run with its
    `startedAt`, `completedAt` and conclusion.
  - MERGED and CLOSED are final: ask once.
  - An open PR is asked again at most once a minute.
  - A failed `gh` keeps the previous answer.
- **`rate`:** across every ask, from the usage of calls made in the last 5 minutes.
- **Times:** epoch ms. A session whose transcript cannot be found is absent from `sessions`, never
  an error. Read `src-tauri/src/mission.rs` (`Tally`, `transcript_in`, `account_dirs`) before
  writing a reader of your own.

## ship

- **files:** src/nexus/ship.ts, src/nexus/ship.test.ts, src/nexus/log.ts, src/nexus/log.test.ts
- **exposes:** `buildShip(input: ShipInput): Ship`, `logEvents(prev: Ship | null, next: Ship, now: number): LogEvent[]`
- **consumes:** nothing
- **model:** opus
- **effort:** high

The fleet as a ship. It is pure: the same input gives the same ship. Use v1's `classOf`,
`accessoryOf` and `labelOf` (`world.ts`), and `injectedRules` (`packets.ts`).

- **Busy:**
  - The answer is `agents[sessionId]`, the fleet's state fused with hook events (v1 read `tempo`
    and drew working children idle).
  - The snapshot's state, tempo and `waiting_for` only fill in for a session the fleet does not
    list.
  - A tool call still awaiting its result (`activity.running`, under 3 h) is working.
- **State, zone, line, act, twins, emblems, deck order, docks, exception order, `hangarReady`,
  counts:** exactly as the spec lists them. Keep the prototype's numbers: 150 s, 15 min, 40 min for
  a child without a PR, 60 s to `done`.
- **The PR:**
  - A robot's `ci` comes from its fate's checks at `now`.
  - A robot whose PR merged or closed before `now` is not on the ship.
  - A robot with an open PR stays in the hangar.
- **A grandchild** stands under its top captain with `via`. A child with no recorded parent stands
  under `orphanCaptainId(root)`, pose `empty`.
- **A captain's `name`:** the fleet's tab title, else `claude agents`' name. `notices`: its
  children with `notice: 'held'`.
- **A robot's `need`:**
  - a question: the feed's `ask`, else the snapshot's `needs`;
  - a permission: `waiting_for`, with options `yes` / `always` / `no`.
- **`model`:** the snapshot's, else the feed's, so no robot is grey for want of `--model`.
- **A `land` exception:** for every `landable` mission.
- **`logEvents`:** dispatched, PR opened, CI green or red, merged, closed, finished without a PR,
  stranded, blocked, and a newly injected rule (`rule`). Each id is stable, so the store never logs
  a milestone twice. Times are the change's own when the data has one (`mergedAt`, a check's
  `completedAt`), else `now`.

## scene

- **files:** src/nexus/ShipScene.tsx, src/nexus/ship-scene/, src/nexus/ship.css
- **exposes:** `ShipScene(props: ShipSceneProps): JSX.Element`
- **consumes:** nothing
- **model:** opus
- **effort:** high

The ship drawn, as the prototype draws it, with React and DOM sprites from `SHIP_SPRITES` and v1's
`SPRITES` at integer scales, `image-rendering: pixelated`.

- **The top bar:** counts as chips. `! N need you` calls `onTriage`. A rate chip shows calls/min and
  output tokens/min, with input and cache share on hover.
- **The bridge:**
  - the exception rows, labelled ⌥1…⌥9, each with its buttons, calling `onAction`;
  - the blocked robots, each with its question and an inline answer;
  - the `bridge` slot (the fleet prompt);
  - its tone (red, lilac, calm).
- **Decks:**
  - The command room holds captains: busy types, idle crosses its arms, alert stands and points,
    offline slumps, and an orphan is the empty chair. Each shows its name, topic, action and ✉
    notices.
  - Rooms by weight: in flight ≈56%, hangar ≈29%, stranded ≈15%. Lights on when occupied.
  - The hangar folds into a stack when it runs out of floor, and glows while `hangarReady`.
  - The docks are dimmed until hovered.
- **Motion:**
  - Robots walk between rooms (they face where they go).
  - A new robot beams in on the teleport pad.
  - A merged one boards its shuttle and launches; a closed one walks off.
  - Particles follow `action` (sparks, bubbles, pages, zZ, radar).
  - ◈ flashes on a new `injected` rule.
  - Speech bubbles: as many per room as its width allows (~240 px each). The freshest line speaks
    first, the rest take turns every ~4.5 s.
- **Hover:** a robot's full line, model, context, contract relations and its twin as labelled dotted
  lines, and its injected rules. A captain hovered ties itself to its crew.
- **`focusId`:** magenta glow under a bouncing ▼, scrolled into view. **`selection`:** dashed.
  Shift+click calls `onSelect`; a click calls `onOpen`.
- **The ship's log:** a one-line ticker of the newest of `log`, expanding to the list. A row opens
  its robot.
- **Reduced motion:** no walking, flights or loops. **Hidden window:** everything pauses.

## command

- **files:** src/nexus/command/
- **exposes:** `runAction(action: ShipAction, ship: Ship): Promise<ActionResult>`, `parseCommand(line: string, ship: Ship): Parsed`, `FleetPrompt(props: FleetPromptProps): JSX.Element | null`, `Triage(props: TriageProps): JSX.Element | null`, `SelectionBar(props: SelectionBarProps): JSX.Element | null`
- **consumes:** nothing
- **model:** opus
- **effort:** high

Everything the Nexus does to the fleet, through the app's existing paths and never a new way to
reach a child. Each result says what happened in English and logs it as `you`. `open` is the view's,
never `runAction`'s.

- **`answer`:**
  - a question goes through `answerQuestion` / `answerQuestionOther` (`src/mission/ask.ts`);
  - a permission goes through `answerPrompt` (`src/cockpit/approve.ts`);
  - both on the child's account.
- **`merge`:** the cockpit's checks-gated merge (`mergePr`, `src/cockpit/actions.ts`), one PR at a
  time, reporting each.
- **`land`:** `mnemo land` on the mission's contract, as an app job.
- **`stop`:** `claude stop` on the child's account (`stopCmd`), never a kill.
- **`message`:** the child's inbox (`mission_reply`).
- **`dispatch`:** `dispatchIssues` (`src/github/actions.ts`).
- **`parseCommand`:**
  - Verbs: `/dispatch <repo> #<n>…`, `/msg <#issue[·twin] | captain | all> <text>`,
    `/merge green`, `/stop all`, `/land <feature>`, `/yes`.
  - The batch verbs (`/merge green`, `/stop all`, `/msg all`) ask for `/yes` first.
  - Anything else is a hint, never an action.
- **`FleetPrompt`:** its input lives in an element with `data-fleet-prompt`. Under it go the hint
  and the last result. A pending confirmation expires after 20 s.
- **`Triage`:** one card per bridge exception that needs a decision. Each shows the robot, its
  breadcrumbs and title, the question, its last lines and its options.
  - Keys: `1`–`9` and `Y`/`N` answer, `←`/`→` move, `T` opens the robot (`onOpen`), `Esc` closes.
  - It closes itself when none is left.
- **`SelectionBar`:** *merge N green · stop N · open N · clear*, for the selected robots, while there
  is a selection.
