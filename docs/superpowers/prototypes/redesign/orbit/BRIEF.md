# Orbit: the space is the attention

Read `../README.md` first (what was asked, what is wrong today, the rules every concept
keeps) and `../DATA.md` (the data). This file is the direction; how to build it is yours.

## The idea

The whole window is one scene, and where a thing stands in it says how much it needs him.
At the centre is mnemo's memory: the central piece of the brand, drawn in thin white line,
alive. Around it, every parent session is a body in orbit. A session that needs him falls
inward; one that is working holds the middle; a quiet one drifts out; an ended one fades at
the edge. Its children circle it as moons. When mnemo hands a session a rule, a mote of
light travels from the centre to that body, and he sees memory being used.

This is the direction that bets on the first look. Its risk is the one two earlier attempts
died of: a canvas where "position meant nothing" and nobody knew which repo they were in. So
position here is never decoration:

- **distance from the centre is attention**, in bands he can name (needs you, working,
  your turn, ended);
- **angle is the project**, a fixed sector with its name on the rim, so a session lives in
  the same direction all day and never jumps for cosmetic reasons;
- **every body carries its full title**, laid out so that labels do not collide or clip;
- a body's ring is its context filling up; its size is what its tree has spent; its shape
  and motion say its state without colour.

## What it must show

**The scene.** The centre piece, the bands, the project sectors, the bodies with their
moons, the motes of memory in flight (from the capture's `memory.injected` and
`memory.read`, replayed), and a quiet line of fleet figures: how many need him, are working,
are ready; what the accounts have left of their plan windows.

**Focus.** Choosing a body (click, or arrow keys that move between neighbours, or typing part
of its name) brings it forward: the scene yields, and the session opens as a station with
its terminal (drawn as `DATA.md` says), its family, and three readings:

- *Context and tokens:* how full, the curve with its compaction cliffs, what it spent by
  kind, what its sub-agents and its tree spent, the plan windows.
- *Memory:* what it loaded at the start, what it touched of Claude Code's memory, the
  briefings, the rules injected with scores, the rules it asked for, and what mnemo
  considered and held back, with the reason.
- *Sight:* pages with the screenshots it took, searches and their sources, fetched
  documents, artifacts, files, pull requests.

Esc always returns to the scene, and the scene is where he left it. Moving from one focused
session to the next does not pass through the scene.

**Pending.** A session that needs him is unmissable in the scene without sound or a popup:
it is closer, and it moves differently. Its question opens on the body itself, with its
choices as keys; a merge is approved there. One key flies to the next pending item, and
answering the last one visibly settles the scene.

**The centre.** Memory is not only a backdrop: opening the centre shows what the vault holds
and what mnemo did lately (rules, what it learned, how often it spoke and how often it stayed
silent and why).

## What would make it the one he picks

Opening the app in the morning and knowing, before reading a word, who needs him and where
the work is. And wanting to leave it on the second monitor because it is beautiful.

The motion rules of `../README.md` bind hardest here: at rest, with nothing changing, the
scene costs nothing on the main thread.
